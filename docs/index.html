Telltale

Take a photo of your dashboard. Telltale finds each lit warning light and tells you what it means.

  • Amber: have it checked soon
  • Green or blue: information

How it works

  1. 01

    Find

    It looks for bright, colorful spots, the way a lit icon stands out on a dark panel, and joins the pieces of each icon together.

  2. 02

    Crop

    Each spot is cut out as a small square image, so every light is judged on its own.

  3. 03

    Classify

    A small neural network (MobileNetV3) names the light, or says it is unsure. It learned from generated images of standard symbols in many styles, colors and lighting conditions.

Everything runs in your browser: the photo never leaves your device.

Why this exists

Even though there are many drivers out on the road, some may not have taken the time to sift through their owner's manual, and some may not know the meaning of every symbol on their dashboard. To provide quick answers to safety concerns without needing to immediately consult the manual or describe a dashboard icon to a search engine, Telltale offers an intuitive way to figure out what a symbol could mean. Start by uploading a photo, and it will identify each symbol and its urgency. Of course, AI can be wrong, so check anything it marks as "possible" or "unsure" against your owner's manual.

What it knows

Any other light (for example traction control, seat belt or engine temperature) is shown as "unsure" or "probably not a warning light". Your owner's manual lists every light in your car.

Limits

This is a learning project, not a safety tool. On the dashboard photos it was tested on, the CNN named about 3 in 4 lights, and when it said "identified" it was right every time. Those photos also guided its design, so expect lower results on cars it has not seen.

  • Bright daylight washes out the colors of the lights, so it can miss them: use "Draw a box".
  • It knows 12 common lights. Anything else is shown as unsure.
  • Blurry, angled or very dark photos make every step less reliable.

About this project. Telltale is a learning project about how a machine-learning product is made from start to finish: generating training data, training and testing a model, and running it privately in your browser.
